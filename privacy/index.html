Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who uses this app

The General is a private app used only by the members of one household, for their own accounts. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app reads email in the household's own Google account through the Gmail API: messages, their labels, and attachment names. It may apply labels to help organize that mail.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features to the household itself: filing and summarizing its own mail, reminders, and answering its own questions about its records.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored only on the household's own computer. It is not stored on any server operated for this app.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models. People do not read it except the household members themselves.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The General's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted at the account owner's request.

Contact

Use the support contact shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
